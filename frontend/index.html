📄 Research Paper

"Data driven water quality assessment using machine learning and synthetic data generation"

Journal: Scientific Reports (2026)

DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-54560-7

🏆 Key Findings

  • XGBoost & GB achieved 99.47% on SMOTE-balanced data
  • SMOTE outperformed MAHAKIL and GAN for oversampling
  • SMOTE MMD score of 0.0067 (excellent synthetic data quality)
  • 10-fold cross-validation used for robustness
  • 3 datasets evaluated across 4 ML models

⚙️ Methodology

  • Min-Max normalization (Eq. 1 from paper)
  • Mean imputation for missing values
  • 80/20 stratified train-test split
  • SMOTE applied to training set only (prevents data leakage)
  • 10-fold cross-validation on training set

📊 Dataset Info

  • 3,276 total water samples (Kaggle)
  • 1,998 Non-potable | 1,278 Potable
  • 9 physicochemical features
  • Missing values in pH, Sulfate, Trihalomethanes
  • Mean imputation used for missing values

ℹ️ About AquaPredict

Science-backed water quality prediction for public health.

What is AquaPredict?

AquaPredict is a machine learning-powered web application that predicts water potability based on physicochemical parameters. It implements the methodology from peer-reviewed research published in Scientific Reports (2026), using an ensemble of Random Forest, XGBoost, Gradient Boosting, and Logistic Regression models trained on real-world water quality data.

The model was trained on 3,276 water samples from the Kaggle Water Potability dataset, with SMOTE oversampling to address class imbalance (1,998 non-potable vs 1,278 potable samples), following the exact methodology proven to achieve 99.47% accuracy in the reference study.

Important Disclaimer

⚠️ This tool is for educational and research purposes only. Predictions should NOT replace certified laboratory water quality testing. Always consult certified water testing authorities before making decisions about water consumption. WHO standards and EPA guidelines should be followed for official water quality determination.
